## Style and voice notes

- Never say "PWA" or "Progressive Web App" to attendees. Just "app".
- The emphasis on **Safari** (iPhone) and **Chrome** (Android) is non-negotiable. People default to whatever browser last opened a link, and iOS only installs home-screen apps from Safari.
- Full URL `https://2027vendorday.gatewayscte.org` is long for print. Drop the `https://` prefix in printed materials — the QR carries the real link, and most browsers add it back automatically.
- Mirror the app's icon and brand color in printed materials so attendees recognize the installed home-screen icon after setup.
- The app works offline for cached content (agenda, sponsors, map, recap), so the "works without signal" line in Format 1 is accurate for the venue's weaker Wi-Fi corners.

## Troubleshooting lines (optional, for FAQs or help desk card)

- **"It opened in Chrome on my iPhone and I can't install it."** → Copy the URL, open **Safari**, paste it there, then tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**.
- **"I don't see an Install button on Android."** → Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home Screen**.
- **"The icon says 'SCTE Vendor Day' — is that right?"** → Yes. That's the installed app.
- **"The app looks blank or won't load."** → Pull down to refresh, or close and reopen it. If you're on venue Wi-Fi, try switching to cellular.
